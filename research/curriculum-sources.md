## Alignment: OpenStax Vol 1 to AP World History

OpenStax Volume 1 (to 1500) aligns primarily with AP Units 1-2 (1200-1450).
The earlier material (prehistory through 1200 CE) has no direct AP counterpart
since AP World History: Modern starts at c. 1200 CE.

| OpenStax Part | AP Unit | Overlap |
|---|---|---|
| Part 1: Early Human Societies | -- | Pre-1200, not in AP Modern |
| Part 2: States and Empires, 1000 BCE-500 CE | -- | Pre-1200, not in AP Modern |
| Part 3: An Age of Religion, 500-1200 CE | -- | Pre-1200, not in AP Modern |
| Part 4: A Global Middle Ages, 1200-1500 CE | Unit 1 + Unit 2 | Strong overlap |

For curriculum generation, OpenStax provides the primary structure for the
ancient and early medieval periods. AP topics are useful for the 1200-1500
period and for cross-referencing thematic organization.

## Notes for Curriculum Generation

1. **Hierarchy mapping**: OpenStax Part -> Area, Chapter -> Topic, Section -> Concept
2. **Date ranges**: Part 1 (prehistory-3000 BCE), Part 2 (1000 BCE-500 CE),
   Part 3 (500-1200 CE), Part 4 (1200-1500 CE)
3. **Learning objectives** serve as concept descriptions for curriculum nodes
4. **63 content sections** map to ~63 curriculum leaf nodes, with chapter intros
   providing the topic-level descriptions
5. **Cross-curriculum connections**: Ancient Greece, Rome, Persia, Islam,
   Byzantine Empire, Mongols, trade routes all connect to existing Petrarca
   curricula (Sicily, Rome, Greece, Byzantine+Islamic)
6. **AP thematic lenses** (trade networks, state building, belief systems)
   can enrich the concept descriptions with analytical framing
